#### Free Thinking
- For the time being I will refer to my game as hide and seek while the game is still pending
- [Hide and seek] will be an asymmetrical top down experience between two players
- One player hunting the other, whose main objective is just to survive
- Whoever is seeking’s goal will be to both locate and close the distance with the opposition
- Whoever is hiding will be trying to stall out for time or complete objectives that have yet to be determined 
- Everything takes place within a randomly generated map divided across a grid
- Each role will have three sets of classes, abilities and determine their movement across the board by the role of a dice
- The hunter will only be able to see around themselves in a 5x5 grid
- Debeating whether only the hider will have walls in this maze, maybe the hunter will just see a blank playing space they can travel through freely
- One class could be the torch bringer, who can place torches to light 3x3 areas on the map
  - Maybe they can teleport themselves to the placed toches
- One class can draw lines in the board to light it and or create walls the other player cannot pass through
- Should not be a tight, wall to wall maze, should be more of an interconnected set of rooms
- There might be a card system to impose buffs and debuffs on both players
  - Hunter can only see 3x3 around them for a temporary period of time
  - Hider could phase through one wall of choice to get to the other side
  - Add values to your movement role or take away from your enemy
  - Can hide the hiders appears for one movement turn of the hunter
- You can only carry three cards at a time, but you can only use two in one turn
- The players will move by the role of a die, the hunter will have higher values by default than the hider
